# Booking-v3 date hint conflicts with day-first interpretation — 33 reports from #4471

**Coverage: 33 unique report IDs** in the 2026-10-04 export, all form reports: Alder Physio 12, Copper Studio 11 and Pale Harbour 10. Exact IDs and distributions are in [the coverage ledger](../evidence/report-coverage.json), group `hint`.

## Problem and evidence

Booking-v3 displays **MM/DD/YYYY**, but its form reads dates in the day-first tenant's convention. The hint and accepted format should agree. Following the hint can reverse the intended month and day. The mechanism is documented in `support-kb/src/lib/kb/causes.ts` (`DATE_HINT` and `causeOf`), distinct from the shared month-first parser.

- R-41230, Copper Studio form, describes 6 January becoming 1 June. Its intake tag says `parser`; the tenant's es-ES / booking-v3 / form path yields `hint`.
- R-41584, also Copper Studio form, describes 7 November becoming 11 July.
- The affected tenant settings are de-DE, es-ES and pt-PT on booking-v3. Their app/email reports still follow the parser path. Neither tenant nor channel alone identifies this problem.

The reports do not preserve each original typed string. The exported model supplies the input-path explanation; production reproduction and historical tenant settings remain to be checked.

## Count and boundaries

Deduplicate by ID before joining tenant settings. The original classifier gives 379 parser / 33 hint. The revised ticket allocation is **374 parser + 33 form hint + 5 date-shift investigation = 412**, with no overlap. The five desk-flagged reports are all outside this hint group.

This group has 18 `hint` intake tags and 15 `parser` tags. Received dates span **2026-04-17–2026-09-22**, preserving the source date portions. R-41230 was received `2026-04-17T22:44:00+02:00` but merged `2026-07-02T09:56:00+02:00`; the merge date is not the first observation.

Sources: original `reports-4471.json`, tenant directory, `causes.ts` and `locales.ts` under `support-kb/src/lib/`; `kb/problems.ts` computes the proposed exclusive coverage.

## Engineering follow-up and acceptance criteria

1. Reproduce booking-v3 form submissions for the three tenant configurations, retaining entered text, interpreted date and output.
2. Make the displayed hint and accepted input convention agree. Verify ambiguous dates, validation and the resulting stored/displayed date.
3. Confirm booking-v2 and app/email paths retain their intended behavior.
4. Verify this correction independently from [the parser issue](01-parser.md) and [the date-shift investigation](03-day-shift.md).

No fix has been implemented or verified. This is a prepared issue for the supplied snapshot. [The signed KB page](../support-kb/src/routes/kb/decisions/0002-triage-by-cause/+page.svx) explains the complete allocation; [its rendered version](../rendered/page.html) is included.
